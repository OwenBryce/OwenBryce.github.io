BIOGRAPHY

The Origin

Owen Bryce is a singer, songwriter, and producer who’s always seeking adventure with good friends. His warm, dreamy music is full of meaning. Rooted in indie folk, his sound exudes freedom, nostalgia, and a touch of melancholy. Each song feels like a gentle breeze, inviting you to pause and feel.

Currently studying at the Rock Academy, Owen continues to refine his sound and grow as an artist. He draws inspiration from the stories people share with him and his own experiences, weaving them into his music in a unique and heartfelt way. Through authentic storytelling, he touches his listeners and takes them on a dreamy journey.

Whether you're lost in the city or free under the stars, his music is for those who dare to feel, and long to go on an adventure with him.
